This package records the prospective protocol and frozen author code for a
40-seed internal replication of fixed-queue alarm prioritization. The seeds
are 1000–1039. No data from this seed set were generated or models fitted
during preparation of this package. Publication approval and a verified
public release must precede scientific execution.

The experiment compares six prioritization policies under the same detected
alarm queue, observable scores and channel criticality information. It uses
12 criticality profiles and three reference-event coverage targets. The
independent inferential unit is the generator seed. Four primary contrasts
use the fixed five-model average and Bonferroni-adjusted two-sided 98.75%
intervals; the six-model sensitivity and individual-model intervals are
descriptive and unadjusted. The protocol defines the complete analysis.

This is an internal replication within the same synthetic generator family.
It does not establish external validity, operator benefit, physical damage
reduction, failure prevention or explanation-specific superiority. C1 is a
structural calibration-consistency contrast. Earlier V4 and V5 confirmatory
attempts stopped at property gates without endpoint analysis; their known
metadata identities and limitations are disclosed in the candidate JSON.
Completed discovery experiments and engineering smoke runs are not rerun.
